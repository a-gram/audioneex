About

Who we are

We are a research-driven provider of software and consultations for Automatic Content Identification in the multimedia industry. Our mission is to provide cutting-edge, reliable and cost-effective solutions that are flexible and easy to integrate. We combine passion for anything multimedia with industry experience and AI technologies to create products that help our customers power their ideas.

We are headquartered Down Under, in the vibrant city of Melbourne, but our team is spread all across the globe. We know no boundaries when it comes to top notch talent.
